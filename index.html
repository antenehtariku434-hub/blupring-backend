01

Body

The structural system. Everything else sits on top of this — energy, focus, and mood all trace back to how well it's maintained.

Load

Progressive overload

Small, consistent increases in weight, reps, or difficulty beat occasional heroic effort. Track your numbers so "progress" isn't a feeling, it's a log entry.

Frequency

Show up more than you skip

Three imperfect sessions a week outperform one perfect session that only happens sometimes. Consistency compounds; intensity alone doesn't.

Recovery

Sleep is part of training

Muscle is repaired at rest, not in the gym. Treat 7–9 hours of sleep as a training input, not an afterthought.

Fuel

Protein and consistency

You don't need a perfect diet, you need a repeatable one — enough protein, mostly whole food, and room to live your life.

02

Mind

The control system. It decides whether the other two systems actually get maintained, especially on the days you don't feel like it.

Systems

Habits over willpower

Willpower runs out by evening. Build routines that don't require a decision — same workout time, same wind-down, so discipline isn't a daily negotiation.

Input

Guard what you consume

What you read, watch, and scroll shapes your baseline mood more than most people admit. Curate it like you'd curate your diet.

Processing

Write it down

A few minutes of journaling turns vague anxiety into a specific, solvable problem. Unprocessed thoughts just loop.

Calibration

Talk to someone real

A therapist, mentor, or honest friend catches blind spots you can't see from inside your own head. This isn't optional infrastructure — it's maintenance.

03

Presentation

The surface finish. Not about chasing a look — about removing friction so how you show up matches the effort you're putting in underneath.

Baseline

Hygiene and grooming

Skin, hair, teeth, nails — unglamorous, but it's the highest-return-on-effort category here. Handle it on a schedule, not when you remember.

Fit

Clothes that actually fit

Fit matters more than brand or cost. A plain t-shirt that fits right will always look better than an expensive one that doesn't.

Carriage

Posture

How you stand and move reads as confidence before you say a word. It's also just better for your body long-term — a rare two-for-one.

Signal

Let the other systems show

Presentation works best as a reflection of the body and mind work, not a substitute for it. It's finish, not foundation.

Custom backend

Daily check-in

Log which system you worked on today. This posts to a real backend server and shows everyone's entries below.
